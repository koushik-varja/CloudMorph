# Third-party dependencies

CloudMorph source is original. It depends on open-source libraries declared in `backend/requirements.txt` and `frontend/package.json`.
No cloud-provider UI, proprietary consultancy asset, customer dataset, or cloud-migration repository is copied into this project.

Key libraries: FastAPI, Pydantic, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, NetworkX, OR-Tools, NumPy, PyYAML, React, TypeScript, Vite, and @xyflow/react. Their respective upstream licenses apply.
